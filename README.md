## 🧩 How to Use Doubt

After you open the application, you will see a blank workspace. This is your canvas for mapping out claims and evidence.

### Creating Your First Map
To start, type your main question into the central node. The application uses AI to suggest related concepts. You can drag these suggestions onto the screen to build a web of information.

### Adding Sources
When you find a source that supports or disputes a point, click the "Add Source" button. Paste the link or the text content here. The application analyzes the text and attaches it to the relevant node in your map.

### Checking Facts
The tool monitors the connection between your sources and your claims. If a piece of evidence contradicts your current map, the software highlights the connection in a different color. This allows you to inspect the data and decide if your claim needs adjustment.

## 🛡️ Privacy and Safety

Your research remains private. The application processes your maps locally on your computer. While the software needs an internet connection to reach specific databases for fact-checking, it does not share your personal research maps with other users.
